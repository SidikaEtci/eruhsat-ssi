How Aries Wallet Verification Works
Issuing a License (Municipality → Citizen)
1
Municipality officer fills the issue form on the main page and includes the citizen's Aries holder DID.
2
The backend creates a W3C Verifiable Credential signed with the municipality's Ed25519 key and logs it to the blockchain.
3
A credential offer is sent to the citizen's wallet. The citizen must accept it — nothing is added to their wallet without consent.
Presenting to an Inspector (Fraud-proof)
4
Citizen clicks Show QR on their credential. The backend generates a one-time challenge string.
5
Aries wallet accepts the proof request and returns a one-time presentation token for this challenge.
6
A QR code is generated containing the challenge ID + holder DID + presentation token. The QR expires in 5 minutes and is single-use.
7
Inspector scans the QR → backend validates holder binding, one-time token, and municipality signature on the VC.
Why This Stops Fraud
✗
Screenshot attack: The QR expires in 5 min and is consumed on first scan — screenshots are useless.
✗
Replay attack: Each challenge is a UUID stored server-side and marked used immediately — the same QR cannot be scanned twice.
✗
Forgery: The one-time presentation token is bound to the exact holder and challenge, so forged holder claims fail verification.
✗
Tampering: The municipality's Ed25519 signature on the VC is re-verified every time — any modification breaks the proof.
